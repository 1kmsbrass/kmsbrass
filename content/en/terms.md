Kumbakonam Metal Stores supplies brass articles for pooja, lamps, temples, vessels, cookware and bulk gifting, subject to item availability and confirmed specifications. This is a wholesale enquiry site: **we do not show retail checkout prices or unverified rates.** Ask for a straight quote for the item and quantity you need.

## What we sell and what we don't

We handle wholesale and bulk enquiries for brass articles such as pooja items, lamps, temple articles, vessels, cookware and gifting requirements. Product-specific pages will publish stable facts such as material, sizes, weights, capacity and minimum order quantity as each specification is verified.

We do not offer trade credit, display v1 public prices, guarantee unconfirmed stock, or promise an availability/dispatch date before the order details are confirmed. We do not accept ritual assumptions as proof: requirements specific to a temple should be confirmed with its committee or officiant. We do not use fake stock counters, discounts or reply-time promises.

## How ordering works

1. Share the article or reference photo, size/variant, quantity, delivery city and required date by WhatsApp or phone.
2. KMS confirms the specification, minimum order quantity (MOQ), availability and the written quote.
3. Before paying, confirm the expected dispatch date, packing, transport responsibility, and the applicable return and transit-damage terms in writing.
4. Wholesale orders require advance payment. **No credit is offered to B2B buyers.**

## Terms in plain words

### Price

Rate follows today's brass rate and your quantity. Message us for a straight number. This site currently hides all price figures until a public value has been verified and approved. A quote should identify the product/variant and the date it applies to.

### Minimum order quantity

MOQ depends on the product and variant. It is not assumed to be one piece; ask us to confirm the MOQ before you plan the order.

### Weight, size and handmade variation

A stated weight or size must be understood as the specification for that variant. Handmade articles can vary. KMS has not published a universal weight tolerance here; ask for the product-specific expected range before confirming an order.

### Dispatch and transport

There is no universal dispatch promise on this page. Confirm the estimated dispatch date, packing method and whether transport is paid by the buyer or included in the quote before paying.

### Returns and transit damage

A blanket return window or transit-damage policy has not been published here. Do not assume that a return or claim will automatically be accepted. Ask for the applicable written terms before payment, and keep the order confirmation and delivery records.

### Payments and credit

Wholesale orders require advance payment. KMS does not offer credit to B2B shops. Do not dispatch goods or make a payment based on an unconfirmed verbal assumption; get the agreed order details and amount in writing.

### Visiting the shop

Opening hours are not published yet. Please call before visiting. Time zone: India Standard Time (IST, Asia/Kolkata). Holiday hours may vary; confirm before travelling.
