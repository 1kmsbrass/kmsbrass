# We Don't Exploit Bhakti. We Enable It.

People buy lamps, kalashas, bells, plates and other articles because these objects have a place in their worship and traditions. That deserves respect.

**We don't exploit bhakti. We enable it.**

Bhakti is not a sales lever. A person's devotion should never be used to pressure them into spending more, create fear, or make them believe that a product will secure divine favour.

Our role is straightforward: help people find suitable articles for their practices, explain what we are selling honestly, and handle their orders with care.

## What we don't do

- We don't use fear, guilt, superstition or religious urgency to push a sale.
- We don't promise blessings, prosperity, protection, remedies or spiritual results from buying a product.
- We don't invent claims about consecration, temple origin, ritual approval, purity or authenticity.
- We don't pretend to be religious authorities or tell customers what their worship requires.
- We don't hide material, weight, dimensions, finish, variation or other relevant product details.
- We don't disguise ordinary products with grand spiritual language to justify a higher price.
- We don't create false scarcity, artificial discounts or pressure around auspicious dates.

## What we do instead

- Describe the product, material, dimensions, weight, finish and intended use as accurately as we can.
- Distinguish verified facts from traditional practices, beliefs and details we cannot confirm.
- Acknowledge handmade variation and explain it rather than hiding it.
- Use real product photographs and make the actual item clear.
- Pack and dispatch products carefully, recognising the importance they may have to the person receiving them.
- Ask customers to confirm tradition-specific requirements with their family, temple committee or officiant when appropriate.

## Respect without pretending

We do not need to claim spiritual powers or religious authority to respect an object used in worship. We can show that respect through honest information, careful selection, responsible handling and straightforward service.

We are sellers. Our responsibility is to be trustworthy sellers.

**We don't exploit bhakti. We enable it — by making the practical side of worship easier, clearer and more dependable.**
