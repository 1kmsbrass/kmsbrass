The product may be the same. **The risks are different.**

When you buy one brass article for your home, you may need help choosing the right size, finish and quality. You want it to arrive safely and look the way you expected. If it does not, the disappointment is yours—and helping you avoid that is part of the work.

## 1. Retail is more than handing over a product

A retail buyer may not know every difference between grades, weights, finishes or handmade details. Some pieces are more delicate. A handmade surface may not be perfectly uniform. A lower price can mean a different level of finish or durability.

We have to help you understand those differences, check that the product suits your purpose, and reduce the chance of an unpleasant surprise. That takes time, care and responsibility.

## 2. Wholesale buyers know the trade-offs

Experienced shop owners often know the product, the market price and what their customers will accept. They understand where a lower price means a compromise, and which small imperfections are normal in handmade work.

They are buying for resale and have their own judgement. Our job is to supply the agreed quality so their investment can earn the return they planned for. We should not undermine that relationship by treating every kind of order as if its risks were identical.

## 3. A good relationship changes the conversation 🤝

If you are a retail customer who wants to buy regularly, tell us openly what you need, what matters most and what you can spend. We can discuss the options and offer a very good price where the order allows it.

We value a lasting relationship more than squeezing a few extra percentage points from one sale. We are a family-owned business, not a listed company reporting growth every quarter. People matter to us in a practical, everyday way.

## 4. If it is for your function, tell us

Buying for a wedding, ceremony or family event is not just another purchase. If something matters to the success of your event, tell us. We will do what we reasonably can to help you get the right items and make the occasion go well.

## The simple answer

**Retail prices can be higher because the responsibility and risk are different—not because the product must be different.**

Speak to us honestly about your needs. We will speak honestly about the options, the quality and the price. That is how we prefer to do business. ❤️
