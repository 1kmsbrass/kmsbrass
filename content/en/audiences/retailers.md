Retail buyers need clear product details and an order process they can plan around. Tell us which brass articles you sell, the variants you need and where the goods should be delivered.

## What to include in a trade enquiry

- Product names or a photo/reference of the article.
- Size, weight or capacity requirements, where known.
- Expected quantity per variant.
- Delivery city and the date you need the order.

## Before confirming an order

Ask us to confirm the available variant, minimum order quantity, packing, transport and exact price. Public rates are indicative only; the final quote depends on the selected product and order details.

**Payment terms:** wholesale orders require advance payment. We do not offer credit to trade buyers.
