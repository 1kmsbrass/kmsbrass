A Kumbabishegam or temple event may involve several articles, multiple quantities and a fixed delivery date. Start with the list you have; we can clarify the product variants and quote requirements item by item.

## Prepare an event enquiry

- Temple name, town and event date.
- The organiser's item list or a reference photo.
- Quantity and size/weight requirements, if known.
- Delivery destination and any packing requirements.

Where the list is still being prepared, confirm ritual requirements with the temple committee or officiants. The product list on this website is not a substitute for temple-specific instructions.

**Plan lead time:** tell us your required date early so transport and availability can be confirmed before the order is accepted.

**Payment terms:** advance payment is required; trade credit is not offered.
