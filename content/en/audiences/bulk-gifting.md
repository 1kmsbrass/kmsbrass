Brass gifting works best when the article, presentation, quantity and delivery timeline are agreed before production or packing is arranged. Tell us the purpose and approximate count, and ask for an exact quote for the selected variant.

## Useful details for a bulk quote

- Occasion: return gifts, wedding, corporate or institutional gifting.
- Preferred article, size, finish and packing expectations.
- Quantity and budget range per piece, if you have one.
- Required date and delivery city.

If the gifting plan is still open, describe the recipient and use first. We can discuss suitable product categories without promising a design or quantity before it is confirmed.

**Payment terms:** bulk orders require advance payment. Prices shown publicly, where available, are indicative and are not a substitute for a written quote.
