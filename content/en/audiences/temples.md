Temple requirements can range from routine pooja articles to items needed for a specific observance or renovation. Share the use and the required specifications so the enquiry can be handled clearly.

## Tell us about the requirement

- Temple or institution name and location.
- Pooja, festival or occasion and the date, if applicable.
- Brass articles required and any known size or capacity.
- Quantity, delivery location and contact person.

If you are researching traditional use, distinguish articles documented in a temple's own material from items commonly associated with a ritual. KMS can discuss product sourcing; temple-specific ritual requirements should be verified with the temple or its officiants.

**Payment terms:** wholesale orders require advance payment. Request a written quote before confirming.
